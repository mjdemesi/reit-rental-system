Continuous Disclosure Obligations

As a listed entity on the Nairobi Securities Exchange (NSE), Meridian Horizon REIT is subject to the NSE Listing Rules and the continuous disclosure obligations set out in the Capital Markets Act. This page provides a comprehensive archive of all market announcements, price-sensitive disclosures, and regulatory notifications made by the Trust since listing.

All announcements are released via the NSE's Electronic Information Dissemination System (EIDS) and are simultaneously published on this website to ensure equitable access to information for all market participants.

For Market Participants & Analysts Price-sensitive information is released simultaneously to the NSE and on this website. For enquiries regarding specific announcements, contact the Company Secretary at company.secretary@meridianhorizon.co.ke

Disclosure Categories

  • Price Sensitive: Information that a reasonable investor would expect to use as part of the basis of their investment decision (e.g., acquisitions, disposals, dividend declarations, valuation updates).
  • Financial: Periodic financial results, including quarterly, half-year, and annual statements.
  • Governance: Board changes, committee appointments, AGM notices, and related party transaction approvals.
  • Operational: Occupancy updates, asset enhancements, development milestones, and portfolio metrics.
