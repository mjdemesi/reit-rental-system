Regulatory Filings & Statutory Disclosures

Meridian Horizon REIT is registered with the Capital Markets Authority (CMA) of Kenya under the Capital Markets (Real Estate Investment Trusts) Regulations, 2013. In accordance with these regulations, the Trust is required to submit periodic filings, disclosures, and compliance certificates to the Authority. This page provides public access to our regulatory filings for the benefit of unitholders, regulators, and other stakeholders.

All filings are submitted within the timelines prescribed by the CMA Regulations and are subject to review by the Authority's REIT supervision unit. The Trust maintains an unqualified compliance record since its listing in 2018.

For Regulators & Compliance Officers Official correspondence with the Capital Markets Authority is maintained in the Trust's secure compliance vault and is available for inspection upon formal request. Contact the Compliance Officer at compliance@meridianhorizon.co.ke

Statutory Filing Obligations

Under the CMA REIT Regulations, the Trust is required to submit the following filings on a periodic basis:

  • Quarterly Financial Returns: Submitted within 30 days of quarter-end, including statement of financial position, comprehensive income, and cash flows.
  • Bi-Annual Independent Valuations: Conducted in June and December by CMA-registered valuers, submitted within 60 days of valuation date.
  • Annual Report & Audited Financial Statements: Submitted within 90 days of financial year-end (31 December), audited by approved audit firm.
  • Annual Compliance Certificate: Signed by the Trustees and Manager, confirming adherence to all REIT Regulations, submitted by 31 January each year.
  • Related Party Transaction Disclosures: Reported within 7 days of transaction execution, with independent trustee approval.
  • Material Event Notifications: Reported within 24 hours of occurrence, including changes in trustee, manager, or valuer.
