Commitment to Our Unitholders

At Meridian Horizon REIT, we recognise that transparent, timely, and meaningful communication with our unitholders is foundational to maintaining market confidence and delivering long-term value. Our Investor Relations programme is designed to ensure that all stakeholders — from institutional fund managers to retail unitholders — have access to the information they need to make informed investment decisions.

We maintain an active engagement calendar that includes quarterly earnings presentations, an Annual General Meeting, non-deal roadshows across East Africa, and participation in regional real estate investment conferences. Our management team is committed to accessibility and responsiveness.
