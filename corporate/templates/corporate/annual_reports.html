Annual Report Archive

Meridian Horizon REIT publishes a comprehensive Annual Report each year, prepared in accordance with International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) and audited by PricewaterhouseCoopers Kenya. The Annual Report includes the Chairman's Statement, Manager's Report, audited financial statements, independent valuation summary, corporate governance report, and sustainability review.

Reports are submitted to the Capital Markets Authority and the Nairobi Securities Exchange within 90 days of the financial year-end (31 December) and are made available to unitholders and the public via this archive.

Request a Printed Copy Printed copies of the Annual Report are available to unitholders upon request. Contact our Investor Relations team at ir@meridianhorizon.co.ke or call +254 20 123 4567.

Report Contents

Each Annual Report includes the following sections, prepared in compliance with IFRS and the CMA REIT Regulations:

  • Chairman's Statement: Strategic overview, market commentary, and outlook from the Chairman of the Board.
  • Manager's Report: Detailed operational review, portfolio performance, and asset management activities.
  • Corporate Governance Report: Board composition, committee activities, risk management framework, and compliance statements.
  • Sustainability Review: ESG performance, environmental initiatives, social impact, and GRESB alignment.
  • Independent Valuation Summary: Summary of bi-annual independent property valuations by Knight Frank Kenya and HassConsult.
  • Audited Financial Statements: Statement of financial position, comprehensive income, changes in unitholders' funds, and cash flows.
  • Independent Auditor's Report: Audit opinion from PricewaterhouseCoopers Kenya.
  • Trustees' Report: Report from Stanbic Bank Kenya Trustees Limited on Trust administration.

Interim Reports & Quarterly Updates

In addition to the Annual Report, the Trust publishes quarterly financial results and operational updates. These are available in the Investor Relations section.
