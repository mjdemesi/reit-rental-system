Independent Property Valuations

In accordance with the Capital Markets (Real Estate Investment Trusts) Regulations, 2013, Meridian Horizon REIT commissions bi-annual independent valuations of its entire property portfolio from CMA-registered valuers. These valuations form the basis of the Trust's Net Asset Value (NAV) reporting and are a critical component of our commitment to transparency and unitholder protection.

The Trust engages two leading independent valuation firms — Knight Frank Kenya and HassConsult — to ensure robust cross-validation and minimise concentration risk in the valuation process. Both firms are registered with the Valuation Registration Board of Kenya and conduct valuations in accordance with the International Valuation Standards (IVS).

Valuation Methodology Properties are valued using the Income Capitalisation method for income-producing assets, the Direct Comparison method for residential and retail units, and the Depreciated Replacement Cost method for specialised industrial assets. Capitalisation rates are derived from observable market transactions and adjusted for asset-specific risk factors.

Valuation Governance

The Trust's valuation process is subject to rigorous governance oversight to ensure independence, accuracy, and compliance with regulatory requirements:

  • Valuer Independence: Both Knight Frank Kenya and HassConsult are independent of the Trust, the Manager, and the Trustees. Neither firm has any material business relationship with the Trust beyond the valuation engagement.
  • Rotation Policy: The lead valuation partner at each firm is rotated every five years in accordance with best practice governance standards.
  • Audit & Valuation Committee: The Board's Audit & Valuation Committee reviews all valuation reports, meets directly with the valuers, and challenges key assumptions including capitalisation rates, vacancy assumptions, and growth forecasts.
  • CMA Oversight: Valuation reports are submitted to the Capital Markets Authority within 60 days of the valuation date and form the basis of the Trust's NAV reporting.
  • IVS Compliance: All valuations are conducted in accordance with the International Valuation Standards (IVS) published by the International Valuation Standards Council (IVSC).

Valuer Profiles

  • Knight Frank Kenya: A leading global real estate consultancy with over 25 years of operations in Kenya. Registered with the Valuation Registration Board of Kenya and member of the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors (RICS).
  • HassConsult: A premier Kenyan property consultancy founded in 1992, widely recognised for its authoritative HassConsult Property Index and deep expertise in the Nairobi real estate market. Registered valuers with the Valuation Registration Board of Kenya.
