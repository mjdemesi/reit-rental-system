Investment Strategy

Meridian Horizon REIT is a fully integrated, self-administered real estate investment trust listed on the Nairobi Securities Exchange (NSE: MHR) and regulated by the Capital Markets Authority (CMA) of Kenya. Established in 2018, the Trust has grown to become one of East Africa's premier institutional property investment vehicles.

Our Mission

To deliver sustainable, risk-adjusted returns to our unitholders through strategic acquisition, active management, and value enhancement of high-quality real estate assets across Kenya's most dynamic urban markets. We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of corporate governance, transparency, and environmental stewardship.

Investment Philosophy

  • Quality Assets in Prime Locations: We focus exclusively on Class A and Class B+ properties in high-barrier-to-entry metropolitan nodes with strong demographic fundamentals and limited new supply.
  • Active Value Creation: Through proactive asset management, strategic capital expenditure, and tenant relationship management, we consistently enhance net operating income and property valuations.
  • Disciplined Capital Allocation: We maintain a conservative leverage profile (target LTV ratio of 35-40%) and deploy capital only when risk-adjusted returns meet our stringent hurdle rates.

Portfolio Composition

Our portfolio is strategically diversified across multiple asset classes and geographic regions to mitigate concentration risk and capture growth opportunities across Kenya's real estate landscape.

Asset Class Breakdown

  • Multi-Family Residential (52%): Premium apartment complexes in Nairobi's most sought-after neighbourhoods, targeting middle-to-upper income professionals and expatriates.
  • Commercial Office (28%): Grade A office space in prime business districts including Westlands, Upper Hill, and Central Business District.
  • Retail (12%): Neighbourhood shopping centres and high-street retail locations anchored by established supermarket chains.
  • Industrial & Logistics (8%): Modern warehousing and light industrial facilities along key transport corridors including Mombasa Road and the Northern Bypass.

Geographic Focus

  • Nairobi Metropolitan (78%): Kenya's economic hub and East Africa's commercial centre.
  • Mombasa (12%): Coastal region benefiting from port-driven logistics demand and tourism.
  • Kisumu (6%): Lake Victoria region's growing economic activity under Vision 2030.
  • Nakuru & Eldoret (4%): Agricultural hinterland commercial centres experiencing urbanisation.

Governance & Regulatory Compliance

Meridian Horizon REIT operates under the highest standards of corporate governance and full compliance with Kenyan regulatory requirements. Our governance framework is designed to protect unitholder interests, ensure transparency, and maintain market confidence.

  • Capital Markets Authority (CMA): Full compliance with the Capital Markets (Real Estate Investment Trusts) Regulations, 2013, including quarterly financial reporting, annual valuations by independent valuers, and mandatory distribution of at least 80% of net income as dividends.
  • Nairobi Securities Exchange (NSE): Adherence to all NSE listing requirements, including continuous disclosure obligations and corporate governance code compliance.
  • Tax Compliance: REIT status grants exemption from corporate income tax on rental income distributed to unitholders, subject to compliance with CMA regulations and Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) requirements.
For Regulators & Compliance Officers All statutory filings, annual reports, independent valuations, and audit reports are available in our Investor Relations portal. The Trust maintains an unqualified audit opinion from PricewaterhouseCoopers Kenya and conducts bi-annual independent valuations through Knight Frank Kenya and HassConsult.

Environmental, Social & Governance

We recognise that sustainable value creation requires balancing financial performance with environmental stewardship and social responsibility. Our ESG framework aligns with the Global Real Estate Sustainability Benchmark (GRESB) standards and the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

Environmental Initiatives

  • Energy Efficiency: 68% of our portfolio features LED lighting, solar water heating, and energy-efficient HVAC systems. Targeting 100% LED conversion by 2027.
  • Water Conservation: Rainwater harvesting systems installed in 45% of multi-family residential assets, reducing municipal water consumption by an average of 22%.
  • Waste Management: Comprehensive recycling programmes across all commercial properties, diverting 35% of waste from landfill.

Social Impact

  • Affordable Housing: Committed to allocating 10% of new development pipeline to affordable housing units priced below market rates.
  • Community Engagement: Annual community investment programme supporting education and skills development in neighbourhoods surrounding our properties.
  • Tenant Wellbeing: Comprehensive tenant engagement programmes achieving 94% tenant satisfaction scores.
