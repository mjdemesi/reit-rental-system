Asset Class Performance

The Trust's portfolio is strategically diversified across four primary asset classes, each contributing to overall portfolio stability and growth.

Multi-Family Residential (52% of Portfolio)

  • Occupancy Rate: 97.2% (above portfolio average)
  • Average Rental Growth: 8.5% YoY
  • Tenant Profile: Mid-to-senior level professionals, expatriates, and small families
  • Key Strengths: Strong demand fundamentals in Nairobi's prime residential nodes, limited new supply in established neighbourhoods, and high tenant retention rates (average lease term: 18 months)

Commercial Office (28% of Portfolio)

  • Occupancy Rate: 95.8%
  • Average Rental Growth: 6.2% YoY
  • Tenant Profile: Multinational corporations, financial institutions, professional services firms, and technology companies
  • Key Strengths: Grade A specifications, prime locations with excellent connectivity, and long lease terms (average: 5 years) providing income visibility

Retail (12% of Portfolio)

  • Occupancy Rate: 98.1%
  • Average Rental Growth: 7.8% YoY
  • Tenant Profile: Supermarket anchors, specialty retailers, food & beverage operators
  • Key Strengths: Neighbourhood convenience positioning, strong anchor tenants. Majority of leases are triple-net (NNN) structure

Industrial & Logistics (8% of Portfolio)

  • Occupancy Rate: 91.5%
  • Average Rental Growth: 9.4% YoY (fastest growing segment)
  • Tenant Profile: Third-party logistics providers, e-commerce fulfilment operators, light manufacturing
  • Key Strengths: Benefiting from e-commerce growth and infrastructure development. Strategic locations along major transport corridors

Geographic Distribution

The Trust's portfolio is concentrated in Kenya's most dynamic urban markets, with strategic exposure to both primary and secondary growth centres.

Nairobi Metropolitan (78% of Portfolio)

  • Westlands (32%): Nairobi's premier commercial and residential node, home to multinational headquarters and upscale retail.
  • Upper Hill (24%): Emerging as Nairobi's new CBD, with significant Grade A office development.
  • Kilimani & Lavington (14%): Established high-end residential neighbourhoods with strong expatriate demand.
  • Ruaka & Gigiri (8%): Rapidly developing residential and retail markets benefiting from proximity to UN Complex.

Coastal Region — Mombasa (12%)

Kenya's second-largest city and home to East Africa's largest port. The Trust's coastal portfolio focuses on logistics properties serving the port corridor and commercial properties benefiting from tourism-driven demand.

Western Region — Kisumu (6%)

Emerging commercial centre serving the Lake Victoria region. Benefiting from infrastructure investment under Vision 2030 and growing cross-border trade.

Rift Valley — Nakuru & Eldoret (4%)

Agricultural hinterland commercial centres experiencing urbanisation-driven demand for modern retail and residential space.
